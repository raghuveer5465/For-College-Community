Rules & Guidelines

Follow these simple rules to keep College Share Hub safe, respectful, and useful for everyone.

01

Verified Users Only

Use accurate information and keep your account details private.

02

Use Items Carefully

Borrowed items must be handled responsibly and returned in good condition.

03

Return on Time

Return items by the agreed deadline or request an extension beforehand.

04

Be Honest

Do not create fake listings, false claims, or misleading reports.

05

Respect Everyone

Harassment, abuse, fraud, and sharing private information are not allowed.

06

Report Problems

Report lost items, damage, fake listings, or overdue returns honestly.

If rules are broken: users may receive a warning, lose borrowing access, or have their account suspended after admin review.
